## One deliberate deviation from the ticket's literal wording

The ticket's glossary spec said the new entry should describe
`fair_joint_conservative` as "assumes the legs are independent." That is
false of the actual code: `backend/core/ladder.py::_joint` computes
`conservative` through `joint_probability_all` over `_correlation_leg`
legs -- a seeded Gaussian copula with a same-night correlation nudge --
not a bare product (the bare product is a separate, unused-here `naive`
field, and also what `_prefix_chances` draws for the ladder chart, per its
own docstring). The repo's own `joint_chance` glossary entry already says
"the number here is computed with that link included, not by bare
multiplication." Writing "assumes independence" into a new glossary entry
would have shipped a claim this repo's own measurement rules exist to
prevent. I wrote `chance_when_priced`'s definition to say it is "the same
computation as joint chance" instead -- accurate, and consistent with the
existing entry, at the cost of not using the ticket's exact wording.
